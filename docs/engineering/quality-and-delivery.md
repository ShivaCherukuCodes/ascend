# Quality and delivery

Before release, confirm that the change is understandable, accessible, secure for its data sensitivity, and recoverable if it fails.

## Minimum release questions

- Does this make a person's next step clearer or their experience more trustworthy?
- Are recommendation reasons, consent, and user controls visible where relevant?
- Have important success, empty, error, and interrupted states been designed and tested?
- Is telemetry useful without collecting unnecessary personal content?
- Is the relevant documentation and ADR record current?

Speed matters, but only when it preserves confidence and leaves the system easier to evolve.
