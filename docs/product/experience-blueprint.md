# Experience blueprint

## The feeling

Ascend should feel premium, calm, and quietly optimistic. It should reduce mental load, never create it. The product speaks plainly, makes uncertainty visible, and leaves the person in control.

## Daily rhythm

1. **Arrive:** Today acknowledges the person and shows the most useful focus.
2. **Understand:** A recommendation includes a short, human reason grounded in the person's stated goals and recent context.
3. **Act:** The next action is small enough to start now and meaningful enough to matter.
4. **Reflect:** The person can capture what happened in seconds, including an imperfect day.
5. **Adapt:** Atlas and the other coaches adjust plans with consent, treating missed actions as information rather than failure.

Coaches share one respectful memory and voice, but each must stay within its domain. Ascend presents one coherent plan rather than asking the person to reconcile competing advice.

## Interaction standards

- Prefer one meaningful choice over a busy dashboard.
- Explain recommendations in everyday language and identify the context used.
- Make edit, dismiss, defer, and feedback paths easy to find.
- Use celebration sparingly and sincerely; never weaponize a missed streak.
- Clearly distinguish observation, suggestion, and high-stakes guidance.
