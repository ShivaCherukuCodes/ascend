# User journeys

## First week: from context to trust

1. A person chooses the areas of life they want help with and shares only the context they are comfortable providing.
2. Ascend reflects back what it understood and invites correction.
3. Today offers a small first plan with reasons, not a wall of tasks.
4. The person completes, changes, or defers an action and can say why.
5. Ascend adapts its next suggestion and shows the connection between feedback and change.

## A disrupted day: progress without punishment

1. A planned workout or task is missed.
2. Ascend offers neutral options: reschedule, replace with a lighter action, or let it go.
3. The plan updates without breaking a streak or framing the person as behind.
4. Journey preserves the broader pattern so one day does not eclipse real progress.

## Long-term planning: turning intention into a journey

1. The person describes a goal in their own words.
2. Compass turns it into milestones and a flexible plan for confirmation.
3. Domain coaches contribute recommendations that explain trade-offs.
4. The person revisits the plan over time, with changes recorded as learning rather than failure.
