# Product roadmap

This roadmap is outcome-led. Timing follows learning, safety, and experience quality—not arbitrary feature volume.

## Foundation — make trust possible

- Confirm product language, personas, journeys, and coach boundaries.
- Establish consent, privacy, explanation, and feedback patterns.
- Build the Today experience around one understandable next step.

## Guided daily experience — make it useful

- Onboarding and personal context.
- Plans, check-ins, adaptive daily recommendations, and progress reflection.
- The first integrated coach experiences for selected domains.

## Connected journeys — make it personal

- Long-term goals and cross-domain planning.
- Coach collaboration that resolves trade-offs visibly.
- Richer patterns and insights, always with explanation and user control.

## Enduring companion — make it dependable

- Reliability, accessibility, privacy controls, and continuously improved guidance.
- Deliberate expansion into more domains only where Ascend can be genuinely helpful.
