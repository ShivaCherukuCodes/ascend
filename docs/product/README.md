# Product

Ascend is a personal AI operating system: a calm daily companion that helps a person understand their life, choose a meaningful next step, and grow without judgment.

## Product constitution

1. Understand before recommending.
2. Every click has a purpose.
3. AI acts like a coach who genuinely wants the person to succeed.
4. Progress matters more than numbers.
5. Never punish the user.
6. Celebrate progress over perfection.
7. Explain the reason behind every recommendation.
8. Build for ten years, not ten weeks.
