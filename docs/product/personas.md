# Personas

## The intentional builder

An ambitious adult balancing health, work, relationships, and personal goals. They value structure but are tired of maintaining disconnected tools. Ascend helps them see the whole picture and move one priority forward each day.

## The restarting optimist

Someone returning to routines after a demanding period, setback, or life change. They need a compassionate reset, not a performance score. Ascend makes the smallest useful next step visible and adapts when reality changes.

## The health-aware planner

Someone who cares about training, nutrition, sleep, and recovery but struggles to interpret conflicting signals. They need transparent, practical guidance and appropriate boundaries around medical decisions.

These personas guide prioritization; they are not stereotypes or requirements for a user to fit a fixed profile.
