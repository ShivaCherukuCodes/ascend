# Principles

## Why

Principles turn the [constitution](constitution.md) into a practical review checklist.

## What

Use these questions when reviewing a feature, interaction, data practice, or AI behavior:

1. **Does this preserve the person's ability to choose, decline, pause, or change direction?**
2. **Does this interrupt someone unnecessarily or create pressure to return?**
3. **Does this ask for only the personal context it genuinely needs?**
4. **Would someone understand why this recommendation, prompt, or view exists now?**
5. **Does it make clear what Ascend knows, assumes, and does not know?**
6. **Can a person correct Ascend's understanding, and will that correction take precedence?**
7. **Is the experience simpler and more legible than the alternative?**
8. **Would a difficult day leave the person with dignity intact?**

## How

Answer these questions during discovery, design review, implementation review, and release review. Escalate a conflict to the [constitution](constitution.md); use its priority order rather than inventing a local exception.
