# Mission

## Why

The mission keeps Ascend focused on the purpose it must serve now, before it expands.

## What

Ascend helps people return to their own intentions and make a considered next choice when life becomes fragmented, busy, or uncertain.

Its current focus is not to manage every part of a person's life. It is to make one meaningful moment of reflection and direction easier to return to.

## How

Use the mission to decide whether a proposed capability helps a person reconnect with an intention and choose a next step. Use the [product philosophy](../product/philosophy.md) to evaluate the feature itself.

## Future Considerations

Refine the current focus once the initial audience and recurring moment are approved.

## Open Questions

Human decision required: define the initial audience, recurring moment, and evidence that Ascend is helping.
