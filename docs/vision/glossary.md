# Glossary

## Why

The glossary is Ascend's single authority for approved business vocabulary.

## What

| Term | Definition |
| --- | --- |
| **Ascend** | The product described by this documentation. |
| **Personal Operating System** | A product that helps a person understand their direction and choose a next step across the parts of life they experience as one life. |
| **Progress** | Movement toward a person's own intention. |
| **Recommendation** | Optional guidance that a person may accept, decline, or change. |
| **Correction** | Information a person provides to revise Ascend's understanding of them. |

Unresolved terminology: **Human decision required.**

## How

Add a term only after its business meaning is approved. Keep definitions short, objective, non-promotional, and free of implementation detail. Link to the canonical document when a term has a broader definition, such as trust in the [vision](vision.md).

## Future Considerations

Add approved terms only when the product requires them.
