# Ascend Constitution

Version: Genesis

---

## Article 1

Technology serves humans.

---

## Article 2

Never punish users.

---

## Article 3

Celebrate progress.

Never perfection.

---

## Article 4

Every recommendation explains why.

---

## Article 5

Privacy belongs to the user.

---

## Article 6

Reduce cognitive load.

---

## Article 7

Design calm experiences.

Never overwhelming ones.

---

## Article 8

AI should feel like a mentor.

Not a chatbot.

---

## Article 9

Every interaction should leave users feeling better than before.

---

## Article 10

Before every release ask

Would someone love using this every morning?

If the answer is no,

keep improving.
