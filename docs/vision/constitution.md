# Constitution

## Why

The constitution defines the commitments Ascend must not trade away for growth, speed, or novelty.

## What

1. **Human agency comes first.** The person remains the author of their life.
2. **Privacy is a commitment.** Personal context is not a resource to exploit.
3. **Do no harm.** A difficult day is information, never a reason to shame or pressure someone.
4. **Be transparent.** Guidance must not arrive as unexplained authority.
5. **Honor honest limits.** Ascend never claims certainty, memory, understanding, or authority unless genuinely earned.
6. **Accept correction.** People can correct Ascend's understanding; corrections take precedence over assumptions.
7. **Choose durable simplicity.** Prefer clear, maintainable choices over short-lived shortcuts.

When commitments conflict, use this priority order:

1. Human agency
2. Privacy
3. Non-harm
4. Transparency
5. Simplicity

Every constitutional change requires an [ADR](../decisions/README.md) and explicit approval.

## How

Use the constitution as a hard review boundary for product, design, engineering, and AI decisions. A proposal that violates a commitment is not justified by engagement, speed, or novelty.
