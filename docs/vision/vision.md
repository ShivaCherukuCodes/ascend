# Vision

## Why

Vision defines the destination that should outlast individual products, technologies, and releases.

## What

**Ascend will be the most trusted place for a person to understand their life and choose what comes next.**

In ten years, Ascend succeeds when people return to it not because it demands attention, but because it consistently helps them see their direction, make a considered choice, and remain the author of their own lives.

Trust is earned through clarity, consistency, privacy, honesty, and user control:

- **Clarity:** people can understand what Ascend is showing and why.
- **Consistency:** Ascend behaves reliably across time and circumstances.
- **Privacy:** personal context is handled with care and meaningful choice.
- **Honesty:** Ascend is clear about what it knows, what it does not know, and what it cannot do.
- **User control:** people can disagree, pause, correct, and change direction.

## How

Use this vision to judge whether a long-term opportunity strengthens the relationship Ascend intends to earn. Use the [mission](mission.md) for today's purpose and the [constitution](constitution.md) for immutable commitments.
