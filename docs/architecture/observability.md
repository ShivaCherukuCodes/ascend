## Why
The team must understand product reliability and AI behavior without leaking personal data.
## What
Observability includes structured logs, metrics, traces, dashboards, alerts, and AI quality/cost telemetry.
## How
Define event schemas and redaction rules before instrumenting product flows; connect signals to user outcomes and operational response.
