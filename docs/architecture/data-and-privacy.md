# Data and privacy principles

Personal context is entrusted to Ascend; privacy is therefore a product feature and an architecture constraint.

- Collect only what makes the experience meaningfully better.
- Obtain clear consent for sensitive context and integrations.
- Make it understandable which information informed a recommendation.
- Give people practical access, correction, export, and deletion controls.
- Apply least-privilege access, encryption in transit and at rest, and audit trails for sensitive operations.
- Separate operational telemetry from personal content wherever practical.

Implementation details, retention periods, and compliance obligations will be specified before handling sensitive production data.
